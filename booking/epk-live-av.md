## 2. Artist Positioning & Live Performance Overview

**Manuel Palenque** is an Argentine creative technologist, visual artist, electronic music producer, and live A/V performer with over 15 years of international experience building real-time audiovisual architectures across Tokyo, Hong Kong, Berlin, Venice, Barcelona, and the Americas.

His 45–60 minute live performance (**COLAPSO** / **Syncro A/V**) rejects pre-rendered video playback in favor of a **tightly coupled, real-time generative system** where sound, image, and physical stage lighting are synthesized together in the moment of performance. Built on custom real-time engines (**vvvv**, **TouchDesigner**, **WebGPU**, custom **GLSL/HLSL compute shaders**, and **SDF raymarching**), the system links live electronic synthesis and industrial/experimental club rhythms directly to GPU-accelerated particle physics and venue lighting fixtures via **DMX512 / Art-Net / sACN** and laser control.

### How the Live System Operates
1. **Unified Audiovisual Synthesis (`Syncro A/V` Architecture):** Acoustic transients, sub-bass pressure waves, spectral centroids, and MIDI/OSC state changes do not merely trigger visual clips—they act as physical forces inside a real-time 3D simulation. Sound deforms spatial geometry, drives millions of colliding GPU particles, and modulates shader materiality at 60+ fps.
2. **Spatial Extension via DMX & Lasers:** The visual frame is not confined to the screen. The same real-time parameters driving the projection or LED wall simultaneously control the venue's physical lighting rig (strobes, moving beams, LED bars, and lasers), extending the digital environment into the physical architecture of the room through haze and high-contrast shadow.
3. **Dramaturgy of Order vs. Entropy (`COLAPSO`):** Structurally, the performance unfolds as an escalating tension between algorithmic control and systemic collapse. Beginning with razor-sharp, sub-millisecond perceptual lock (*synchresis*, *temporal binding*, and *rhythmic entrainment*), the harmonic and geometric structures progressively destabilize as the visual and lighting systems assert autonomy—culminating in an immersive, visceral confrontation with digital and physical entropy.
4. **3D Digital Twin Pre-Visualization:** Originally developed to solve zero-rehearsal constraints at massive venues like **Tecnópolis**, Palenque builds a complete **3D Stage Digital Twin** of each festival or club setup prior to arrival—simulating custom screen ratios, LED pixel pitches, sightlines, and DMX fixture universes in software so the show lands locked-in from the first minute of line check.

## 3. Biographies & Curatorial Artist Statement

### Short Bio (100 Words — Ready to Copy for Programs & Flyers)
> **Manuel Palenque** is a Buenos Aires–based creative technologist, visual artist, and live A/V performer with over 15 years of experience at the intersection of real-time computer graphics, electronic sound, and spatial lighting. His performances and interactive installations have been presented at MUTEK, Tecnópolis, SuperDeluxe (Tokyo), the Venice Biennale context (Bright Moments Venezia), School of Creative Media (Hong Kong), NODE Festival (Berlin), and Telenoika (Barcelona). Formerly Technical Video Director for Fuerza Bruta and currently Senior Creative Technologist (Immersive & AI) at Superside, he is the founder of **DataRaíz** and director of the **Ciclo Negro** art-and-technology festival at ArtHaus.

### Full Bio (250 Words — Ready to Copy for Institutional Catalogs & Press)
> **Manuel Palenque** (b. 1986, Buenos Aires) is a visual artist, electronic musician, and Senior Creative Technologist specializing in tightly coupled real-time audiovisual performances, large-scale interactive installations, and 3D stage digital twins. Over a 15-year career spanning South America, Europe, Asia, and the United States, Palenque has engineered systems where generative sound, GPU particle physics, custom shaders, motion capture, and DMX/laser lighting operate as a single performative instrument.
>
> His live A/V works and monumental installations—including *COLAPSO*, *Verano Tecnópolis* (*Bosque* and *Techo*), the *Buenos Aires Planetarium FullDome 360° Interactive System*, and the 45-meter *Coloso* LED tower with DOMA Collective—have reached hundreds of thousands of spectators. Internationally, he has performed and led technical workshops at **MUTEK** (CCK & Matienzo), **Beyond Code #2 at SuperDeluxe** (Tokyo), **TECH LAB PAAK** with the vvvv Japan community (Tokyo), **NODE Forum for Digital Arts** (Berlin), **City University of Hong Kong’s School of Creative Media**, and **Telenoika** (Barcelona). In the large-scale spectacle and commercial realm, he has served as Technical Video Director for **Fuerza Bruta**, directed full A/V and sensor synchronization for Argentina’s **Fiesta Nacional de la Vendimia** (500+ stage performers), developed VR experiences for **Cirque du Soleil’s Messi10 Challenge**, created kinetic retail installations for **Adidas NMD**, and directed global live generative art productions for **Bright Moments** in Venice, Paris, London, and Buenos Aires.
>
> Currently serving as Senior Creative Technologist (Immersive & Creative AI Architectures) at **Superside**—orchestrating systems for clients including Google, Meta, and Reddit—Palenque is also the founder of **DataRaíz** (`dataraiz.io`), an independent Buenos Aires media lab, and creator of **Ciclo Negro** (`ciclonegro.dataraiz.io`), a 12-hour avant-garde festival at ArtHaus.

### Curatorial Artist Statement (English — Translated & Adapted from Original Dossier)
> **My artistic practice is born from friction.** I do not believe in costless art; I seek equilibrium through self-imposed technical and perceptual challenges, transforming obsession with complexity into the indispensable engine of authentic creation. Coming from a Latin American context shaped by structural volatility, mastering real-time technology was never a luxury—it was a vital survival strategy where urgency coexisted with relentless curiosity.
>
> **I view technological progress with pragmatic cynicism.** I reject the industry's sanitized, utopian narrative of "digital democratization"; I know full well that these tools bring crisis, extraction, and obsolescence. Yet rather than retreating from the tide, I choose to ride and subvert it. I deploy artificial intelligence, real-time shaders, and custom code without blind reverence, maintaining a **critical distance**: automating the tedious and **hijacking corporate-grade architectures** to pry open fractures where raw, unpolished human vision can infiltrate.
>
> In live performance, **I pursue a synchronization that aspires to absolute technical perfection, knowing it will ultimately fracture.** I build audiovisual and lighting systems that fight for total mathematical order while embracing failure and overload as core dramaturgical material—because in the end, entropy always confronts us, reminding us that beneath the digital layer, reality remains an uncontrollable phenomenon.
