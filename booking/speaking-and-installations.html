Manuel Palenque — Keynote Speaker Profile & Interactive Installations Dossier (2026/2027)

Bridging Fortune 500 Creative AI & Immersive Systems, Global Theatrical Spectacles, and Avant-Garde Interactive Public Art.

1. Executive Speaker & Curatorial Profile

Manuel Palenque (b. 1986, Buenos Aires) occupies a rare, high-authority intersection in the global creative technology landscape: he executes at the highest enterprise tier while remaining deeply embedded in the experimental, hardware-hacking underground of Latin America.

Over a 15+ year career across Tokyo, Hong Kong, Berlin, Venice, Barcelona, Buenos Aires, and the United States, Palenque has built his practice across three complementary pillars:

  1. Enterprise Creative Technology & AI Leadership (Superside): As Senior Creative Technologist (Immersive & Creative AI Architectures) at global design unicorn Superside, he architects scalable AI and immersive production workflows for Fortune 500 clients including Google, Meta, and Reddit. He is the author of the Immersive Design & Creative AI Architectures methodology (superside-immersive.github.io/course), training creative teams to transition from manual software execution to dynamic system orchestration.
  2. Global Entertainment Spectacles & Monumental Public Art: As former Technical Video Director for Fuerza Bruta (Tokyo / Buenos Aires), Lead A/V & Sensor Synchronization Director for Argentina’s Fiesta Nacional de la Vendimia (500+ stage performers), VR Experience Lead for Cirque du Soleil’s Messi10 Challenge, Global Live Production Lead for Bright Moments (Venice Biennale context, Paris, London, Buenos Aires), and custom LED control engineer for the 45-meter Coloso de Tecnópolis with DOMA Collective, he has engineered mission-critical real-time systems experienced by hundreds of thousands of people.
  3. Independent Curation & Grassroots Media Lab Leadership (DataRaíz / Ciclo Negro): He is the founder of DataRaíz (dataraiz.io), Buenos Aires’ independent laboratory for art, science, and technology, and creator of Ciclo Negro (ciclonegro.dataraiz.io), a 12-hour interactive art and live audiovisual festival at ArtHaus Central.

2. Three Complete Keynote & Lecture Abstracts (2026/2027)

Keynote 1: Syncro A/V — Designing Tightly Coupled Real-Time Audiovisual & Lighting Systems

  • Format: 45–60 min Illustrated Keynote or 90-min Technical Lecture-Demo (with live real-time shader/audio/DMX examples)
  • Reference Portal: https://mpalenque.github.io/syncro-av/

Abstract (200 Words)

Why do certain live performances feel like a single, physical organism while others feel like a concert playing in front of a screensaver? In Syncro A/V, creative technologist and live audiovisual artist Manuel Palenque unpacks the perceptual neuroscience and real-time systems engineering behind tightly coupled sound, image, and spatial lighting. Drawing from 15 years of building high-stakes shows—from directing technical video for Fuerza Bruta and synchronizing 500 performers at Vendimia to performing at MUTEK and Tokyo’s SuperDeluxe—Palenque demonstrates how seven automatic mechanisms of human multisensory integration (synchresis, the ventriloquism effect, the McGurk effect, the temporal binding window, common fate, rhythmic entrainment, and prediction/reward) govern every audience in a dark room. Moving from perceptual theory to live production architecture, the session reveals how to replace pre-rendered timelines with bi-directional state machines linking electronic sound synthesis, GPU particle physics, custom shaders, and physical DMX/Art-Net lighting and lasers. Attendees learn how understanding why sound and image fuse in the brain transforms every technical synchronization parameter into a powerful compositional and dramaturgical decision—including when to maintain sub-millisecond lock and when to let entropy tear the system apart.

Key Takeaways

  1. Perceptual Composition: How to apply the seven principles of multisensory integration (synchresis, temporal binding window, rhythmic entrainment) to design visceral audiovisual fusion.
  2. Unified Signal Architecture: How to architect low-latency, bi-directional pipelines connecting audio feature extraction (FFT, transients, sub-bass pressure) directly to GPU shaders (vvvv / TouchDesigner / WebGPU) and physical DMX/laser hardware.
  3. Dramaturgy of Entropy: How to balance algorithmic precision with controlled systemic failure so live performances remain unpredictable and alive.

Target Audience

Creative technologists, audiovisual artists, live show directors, lighting and stage designers, electronic musicians, experiential agency leads, and media art curators (MUTEK Forum, SXSW Music/Tech, OFFF, university media labs).

Keynote 2: From Stage to WebGPU — Building Scalable Immersive Experiences and 3D Digital Twins for Live Performance

  • Format: 45–60 min Technical & Production Case-Study Keynote
  • Reference Portals: https://mpalenque.github.io/ · https://mpalenque.github.io/curso/

Abstract (200 Words)

In large-scale live entertainment, museum commissions, and international festival touring, the biggest enemy of artistic ambition is time: production teams frequently face complex multi-screen, projection-mapped, or DMX-heavy stages with less than an hour of on-site rehearsal before doors open. In this case-study-driven keynote, Manuel Palenque reveals how 3D Stage Digital Twins, LiDAR spatial scanning, and browser-native WebGPU / WebSockets architectures are transforming how immersive experiences are designed, rehearsed, and scaled. Using real-world post-mortems—including building a full virtual 3D stage and DMX simulation to create his monumental live A/V piece COLAPSO at Tecnópolis in 30 days with zero venue rehearsal; utilizing remote LiDAR scans to calibrate fluid simulations and physical water reflections inside a Renaissance church for Bright Moments Venezia; staging an un-rehearsed 8-hour, 200-piece live generative marathon in Buenos Aires; and deploying a zero-download QR/WebSockets system that turned the Buenos Aires Planetarium into a collective 360° instrument—Palenque provides a battle-tested blueprint for de-risking ambitious spatial productions. Attendees will discover how virtual pre-visualization and distributed web architectures allow small, agile teams to deliver stadium- and museum-grade immersive works on tight budgets and timelines.

Key Takeaways

  1. Zero-Rehearsal Production via Digital Twins: How to build accurate 3D stage digital twins (screens, sightlines, virtual DMX universes, and LiDAR point clouds) to program and stress-test shows before load-in.
  2. Frictionless Crowd Participation at Scale: How to engineer zero-download WebSockets and WebGPU mobile controllers that turn ratusan/thousands of passive spectators into co-creators of a shared 360° projection without Wi-Fi collapse.
  3. Cross-Platform Scalability: Designing modular shader and lighting pipelines that adapt seamlessly from browser previews to 1,200-nit LED walls and fulldome planetariums.

Target Audience

Experiential producers, technical directors, XR/WebGPU engineers, museum and biennial curators, architectural projection specialists, and festival production managers (AWE, SIGGRAPH, SXSW, ISE, LDI).

Keynote 3: Creative Coding & AI Transitions — New Workflows for Interactive Installations and Audiovisual Art

(Alternate Conference Title for Cannes Lions / SXSW: Democratizing Creative AI from LATAM to Global Scale — From Software Users to System Architects)

  • Format: 45–60 min Strategic & Creative Keynote with Live Workflow Demos
  • Reference Portals: https://superside-immersive.github.io/course/index.html · https://superside-immersive.github.io/course/talk.html

Abstract (200 Words)

Artificial Intelligence often feels like a closed black box built by and for the Global North—promising effortless automation while producing homogenized, soulless output. Yet in Latin America, where resource scarcity and "hacking limits" are foundational cultural methodologies, AI and creative coding are being weaponized for radical artistic agency. Presenting a unique dual perspective—as Senior Creative Technologist at global unicorn Superside (building enterprise AI and immersive workflows for Google, Meta, and Reddit) and as founder of the Buenos Aires media lab DataRaíz—Manuel Palenque outlines a paradigm shift from technical execution to dynamic system orchestration. Rejecting both blind techno-utopianism and creative paralysis, Palenque demonstrates how individual artists and lean design studios can use AI coding agents with "critical distance" to build custom real-time pipelines across TouchDesigner, Python, vvvv, custom GLSL shaders, and WebXR. Through live demonstrations and global case studies, this talk shows how adding strict artistic constraints, human taste, and physical-world friction allows independent creators to subvert corporate-grade tools—transforming scarcity into a competitive methodology and empowering creatives to build their own instruments rather than merelyrenting someone else's software.

Key Takeaways

  1. From Software Users to System Architects: A practical methodology for using AI coding agents to bridge TouchDesigner, Python, shaders, and WebXR without decades of traditional software engineering.
  2. Constraints & Friction as Creative Engines: Why AI only becomes artistically compelling when subjected to strict boundaries, physical sensors, real-time performance risk, and human curation.
  3. The Global South Playbook: How to turn resource constraints into high-velocity innovation, redirecting enterprise platforms and workflows to sustain independent art communities like DataRaíz and Ciclo Negro.

Target Audience

Creative directors, innovation leads, brand executives, independent artists, designers, educators, and cultural policymakers (Cannes Lions, SXSW, ODSC AI, OFFF, design schools).

3.1 COLAPSO & COLAPSO 3D Digital Twin (2022–2026)

  • Category: Monumental Audiovisual & Reactive Lighting Installation / Live A/V Performance + 3D Stage Digital Twin
  • Premiere & Venues: Tecnópolis (Buenos Aires, 2022); ArtHaus / Ciclo Negro (Buenos Aires, 2025/2026)
  • Tech Stack: vvvv, GPU real-time particle systems, custom GLSL/HLSL shaders, DMX512 / Art-Net reactive stage lighting, custom 3D Stage Digital Twin simulation software, stereo sub-bass spatial audio.
  • Curatorial Concept: COLAPSO explores the unavoidable progression of entropy and the fragility of human-imposed order. In both its installation and live performance modes, real-time sound and generative GPU particle systems communicate bi-directionally: acoustic energy shapes the visual landscape until the visual and lighting systems progressively destabilize, overpowering harmonic balance and plunging the space into high-contrast digital disintegration.
  • The 3D Digital Twin Innovation: Conceived under an extreme 30-day production sprint for Tecnópolis with virtually zero on-site rehearsal time, Palenque engineered a complete 3D Digital Twin of the venue's screen geometry, architectural sightlines, and DMX lighting fixtures. Every camera angle, luminance interaction, and DMX cue was programmed and rehearsed in virtual space prior to physical load-in.
  • Technical & Spatial Requirements:
    • Display: Fully scalable to custom LED walls (minimum 1,200 nits), large-scale architectural projection mapping (minimum 300 nits surface luminance, ≥ 10,000:1 native contrast ratio), or multi-surface configurations.
    • Environment: Strict ambient light control (total or near-total darkness mandatory to preserve absolute deep blacks) + atmospheric hazer.
    • Audio: Full-range concert PA with clean, high-headroom sub-bass extension (2x balanced XLR/DI stereo inputs).

3.3 FullDome 360° Interactive System (2023–2026)

  • Category: Participatory 360° Fulldome Projection & Multi-User WebSockets Architecture
  • Premiere & Context: Buenos Aires Planetarium / Fulldome 360° Venues (2023)
  • Tech Stack: Custom real-time 360° domemaster rendering engine (vvvv / TouchDesigner / custom shaders), distributed low-latency WebSockets server, zero-download mobile WebApp accessed via dynamic on-dome QR Code projection.
  • Curatorial Concept: Traditional planetarium and immersive dome shows relegate the audience to passive spectatorship. FullDome 360° Interactive System transforms the entire dome into a shared, collective visual instrument. Upon scanning a projected QR code with their smartphones—with zero app download or registration friction—hundreds of spectators simultaneously inject real-time trajectories, forces, and parameter shifts into the 360° generative visual field overhead.
  • Technical Innovation: Specifically engineered to overcome high-density crowd network congestion, the WebSockets state-synchronization layer operates with sub-frame responsiveness even under degraded or offline local Wi-Fi conditions, aggregating hundreds of individual micro-interactions into a coherent, high-resolution 360° visual narrative.
  • Commissioning & Adaptation: Natively adaptable to planetariums, geodesic festival domes, immersive projection rooms (360° wall/floor rooms), and large-scale public/stadium LED facades seeking instant crowd participation.

3.4 Bosque & Techo — Verano Tecnópolis / Electronic Arts Laboratory (2023)

  • Category: Large-Scale Multi-User Interactive Laser, Projection, Fluid & Reactive DMX Environments
  • Venue: Tecnópolis (Feria IT Joven — Laboratorio de Artes Electrónicas, invited by Plan), Buenos Aires (2023)
  • Tech Stack: vvvv, Microsoft Kinect / Depth Sensor Arrays, real-time 2D/3D GPU fluid simulations, synchronized DMX512 / Art-Net moving-head beams, ILDA/DMX lasers, large-format projection mapping, and 3D spatial pre-visualization models.
  • Curatorial & Technical Breakdown:
    • Techo (Interactive Ceiling Fluid & Moving-Light Matrix): An overhead architectural installation synchronizing a matrix of robotic moving-head light beams with a real-time digital fluid simulation projected across the ceiling structure. Every angular sweep and intensity variation of the physical light beams acts as a physical force vector inside the digital fluid solver, while Kinect depth sensors track visitors below—creating a seamless perceptual fusion where physical light beams appear to stir and Agitate liquid digital matter floating in mid-air.
    • Bosque (Site-Specific Interactive Natural-Digital Ecosystem): Installed within a wooded/organic environment at Tecnópolis, Bosque uses Kinect motion capture, volumetric projections, and lasers to suspend responsive digital entities in mid-air without visible screens. As visitors walk through the space, the floating generative organisms react to human proximity and gesture, questioning the imposition of synthetic visual technology onto natural ecosystems.

3.6 Physical-Digital, Shader & Physics R&D Works (Fluids, Colisión, Rope, Agua)

A modular suite of real-time physical simulations and hybrid material studies adaptable to gallery monitors, architectural LED totems, site-specific sculptures, and Times Square / StandardVision urban screens:

Work Title Medium & Tech Stack Curatorial & Technical Description
Agua (Physical Water + Depth Sensors + Projection) Real water basin, Kinect depth sensors, high-contrast video projection, vvvv particle systems (shared engine with COLAPSO). Hybrid physical-digital installation combining real liquid water with depth-tracked generative particle projections. Exploits critical grazing angles and optical refraction on the water's surface to produce an "infinite mirrorless abyss" effect, where physical ripples and digital particle physics amplify one another in real time. (Deployed at scale in Bright Moments Venezia inside a Renaissance church).
Fluids (2D/2.5D Reactive Shader Relief) Custom GLSL/HLSL fragment & compute shaders, real-time 2D fluid velocity maps, normal/displacement texture deformation. Investigates how purely two-dimensional shader math can evoke visceral three-dimensional materiality and relief. A real-time 2D fluid solver continuously deforms surface textures and specular lighting normals, generating liquid-metal and organic topographic illusions at 60+ fps.
Colisión (Generative 3D Particle Sculptures) vvvv, real-time 3D GPU particle collision physics, spatial attractors. A generative sculpture engine where thousands of 3D bodies undergo continuous physical collision, attraction, and repulsion. Emergent, organic three-dimensional forms crystallize out of controlled chaos, evolving infinitely without ever repeating a frame.
Rope (3D Soft-Body & Flexible Material Simulation) vvvv, real-time 3D Verlet integration / constraint physics, PBR material rendering. An experimental R&D study in real-time 3D soft-body physics, simulating the tension, elasticity, knots, and gravitational sag of flexible ropes and fibrous structures responding to virtual and sensor-driven forces.
